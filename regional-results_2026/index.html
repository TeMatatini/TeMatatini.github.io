TE MATATINI O NGĀ TANIWHA

In 2026, 57 kapa haka earned their place at Te Matatini o Ngā Taniwha through 13 regional competitions spanning Aotearoa and Te Whenua Moemoeā. Every rōpū that placed in its rohe qualified for the national stage — hosted by Waikato-Tainui under the mantle of the Kīngitanga.

The draw was made on 8 August 2026 at Tūrangawaewae Marae. All 57 kapa are now placed in one of four pools — Te Ihu, Te Haumi, Te Kei and Te Awa — and drawn across the four days of pool competition. The top three from each pool go through to finals day.

The draw was held on 8 August 2026 at Tūrangawaewae Marae, Ngāruawāhia. All 57 kapa are placed in one of four pools and drawn across the four days of pool competition, 23–26 Pēpuere 2027. The three highest-scoring kapa in each pool advance to finals day on Saturday 27 Pēpuere. The numbers Te Matatini published are each kapa's place in its own pool's draw, shown here under “by pool”. The order of performance on each day, shown under “by day”, interleaves the four pools in pool-number order.

Placings reflect each rohe's official regional results for the 2026 qualifying season (held February–June 2026). Two results were amended after review: Te Arawa confirmed joint regional winners, and Tāmaki Makaurau revised 2nd and 3rd place.
